About the Laboratory

Focused on laboratory care for the local community.

New Bharat Jaanch Ghar has been serving the local area since 2008, providing access to routine and diagnostic laboratory investigations in Runni Saidpur.

The laboratory is located on Hospital Road, Runni Saidpur, Sitamarhi, Bihar, making it convenient for people from Runni Saidpur and nearby areas.
